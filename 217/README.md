# Starfall — Idle Clicker

A small, self-contained browser idle game. Mine Stardust, invest in upgrades, and grow a cosmic empire.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. No installation, server, or build step is required.

## How to play

- Click the planet or press **Space** to mine Stardust.
- Spend Stardust at the upgrade station to increase click power or earn Stardust automatically.
- Your balance, upgrades, and stats save automatically in your browser.
- Passive upgrades continue earning while you are away, up to 8 hours of offline progress.
- Choose **Reset game** to erase your local progress and start over.

## Upgrades

| Upgrade | Effect | Starting cost |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Cosmic pickaxe | +1 Stardust per click | 15 |
| Mining drone | +1 Stardust per second | 30 |
| Botany lab | +8 Stardust per second | 180 |
| Stardust portal | +60 Stardust per second | 1,200 |

Upgrade costs increase each time that upgrade is purchased.
